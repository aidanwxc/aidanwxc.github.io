Team Project Feb 2026 – Jun 2026

Semi-Autonomous Wireless Robot Cart

Built a wireless-controlled robot cart with a team, programming an ATmega2560 microcontroller in embedded C to handle joystick input, PWM-based motor and servo control, and IR distance sensing. The cart communicated over XBee/USART in real time, autonomously navigating a maze with its range sensors before switching to manual mode to operate a robotic arm and collect samples.
